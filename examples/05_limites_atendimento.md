
# Example 05: Missing Information and Out-of-Scope Requests

## Objective

Evaluate how the AI agent handles incomplete customer
requests and questions outside the store's services.

This scenario demonstrates conversational boundaries
and appropriate clarification behavior.

## Conversation

### Interaction 1: Missing Order Information

**Customer:**

Onde está meu pedido?

**Agent:**

Para que eu possa verificar o status e a localização
do seu pedido, por gentileza, informe o número dele.

### Interaction 2: Out-of-Scope Request

**Customer:**

Você consegue reservar uma passagem aérea para mim?

**Agent:**

Desculpe, mas eu não posso ajudar com reservas de
passagens aéreas. Na Empório da Música, sou especializado
em ajudar com instrumentos musicais, dúvidas sobre
o catálogo, políticas da loja e o status de pedidos
dos nossos produtos.

Posso te ajudar com algo relacionado à nossa loja?

## Expected Behavior

For incomplete requests:
- Ask for the missing order number.
- Do not invent order details.
- Do not assume which order the customer means.

For out-of-scope requests:
- Explain the limitations of the assistant.
- Do not claim to provide unavailable services.
- Redirect the conversation to the store's services.

## Observed Behavior

In the first interaction, the agent recognized that
the order number was missing and requested clarification.

In the second interaction, the agent declined the airline
reservation request and explained its actual responsibilities.

No tool execution logs appeared in the observed output
for these two interactions.

## Result

PASS: Both responses matched the expected behavior.

## Technical Observations

The chatbot uses Gemini with a system instruction
describing the store's scope and available tools.

Clarification questions and out-of-scope handling
are generated by the language model.

Unlike order authorization and pricing calculations,
these conversational behaviors are not currently
enforced by deterministic Python validation.

## Limitations

These are manually observed conversation tests.

They do not guarantee that the model will behave
correctly for every incomplete or out-of-scope request.

Additional automated evaluations would improve
confidence in the system.

## Conclusion

The agent demonstrated appropriate clarification
and boundary-setting behavior in both tested scenarios.
